Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly ("we", "us") collects when you use our personal expense tracking service (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly to us. This includes account details such as your name and email address when you register, and the financial information you enter into the Service — expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect limited technical information, such as basic log data, that is generated automatically when you interact with the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and maintain the Service: to store and display your expenses, generate category breakdowns and summaries, and keep your account secure. We may use aggregated, anonymized information to understand how the Service is used and to improve it. We do not sell your personal financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request the export or deletion of your data at any time, and deleting your account will remove your associated expense records from active systems.

4. Third Party Services

We may rely on trusted third-party providers to help operate the Service, such as hosting and infrastructure providers. These providers only process your data on our behalf and are bound to keep it confidential and secure. We do not share your personal financial information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through your account settings. We will do our best to address your concerns promptly.
